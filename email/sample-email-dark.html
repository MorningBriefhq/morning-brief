TOP NEWS
 
01
Fed holds rates steady, puts a December cut back on the table
Chair Powell stressed patience at last month’s meeting but acknowledged “encouraging” progress on inflation, and futures markets now price roughly 60% odds of a quarter-point cut in December. Tomorrow’s CPI report will likely decide it.
02
TSMC’s record quarter keeps the AI trade humming
The chipmaker posted record quarterly revenue and lifted its full-year capital spending outlook, citing unrelenting demand for AI accelerators. The read-through is positive for the whole AI supply chain heading into Big Tech earnings later this month.
03
Gold tops $3,900 as the haven bid builds
Bullion notched another record close on Friday, powered by steady central-bank buying and growing bets on further rate cuts. Silver and platinum followed, with miners among the market’s best performers last week.
04
OPEC+ weighs a November output hike; crude slips
Delegates say the cartel is leaning toward another production increase next month, sending WTI back toward $62 a barrel. Cheaper gasoline is welcome news for consumers — less so for energy-sector earnings this quarter.
05
September jobs report beats forecasts, unemployment steady
Employers added 142,000 jobs last month, above expectations, while the unemployment rate held at 4.3% and wage growth cooled to its slowest pace in two years — the soft-landing mix the Fed wants to see.

YOUR STOCKS
 
NVDA NVIDIA Corporation
TSMC’s record quarter and raised capex outlook are a strong read-through for NVIDIA, with analysts flagging accelerating orders for its latest AI accelerators. Bernstein raised its price target ahead of the company’s November earnings, citing the ramp of the Rubin platform. Hyperscaler capital spending plans, due alongside Big Tech results later this month, remain the key variable for the shares.
AAPL Apple Inc.
Early supply-chain checks suggest iPhone 17 demand is tracking ahead of expectations in the first weeks of the holiday quarter, with the Pro models seeing the strongest sell-through. Services revenue is expected to post another double-digit gain when the company reports later this month. Analysts will be watching for commentary on AI feature adoption as the main driver of the upgrade cycle.
JPM JPMorgan Chase & Co.
JPMorgan kicks off bank earnings season Tuesday before the open, with the Street looking for resilient net interest income and a rebound in trading revenue on elevated market volatility. Credit quality — particularly in commercial real estate and card delinquencies — will be in focus. CEO Jamie Dimon’s outlook commentary typically sets the tone for the whole sector.
